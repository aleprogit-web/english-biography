STEPHEN KING

AUTHOR • STORYTELLER • LEGEND

Stephen King is one of the most influential and successful writers
in modern horror and fiction, known for his gripping stories, complex
characters and unique imagination.

INTRODUCTION

Stephen Edwin King was born in Portland, Maine, in 1947. After his parents separated, he and his older brother were raised by their mother. He graduated from Lisbon Falls High School in 1966 and later attended the University of Maine, where he wrote for the school newspaper and participated in student politics. In 1971, he married Tabitha Spruce, whom he met while they were both students at the university.

CAREER

Stephen King has published dozens of novels, novellas, and short stories, selling hundreds of millions of books worldwide. His career began with Carrie, published in 1974, which became a huge success and allowed him to write full-time. He also published some books under the pseudonym Richard Bachman. Many of his stories have been adapted into movies and TV shows, including Carrie in 1976 and The Shining in 1980.

MUSIC

Outside of writing, King is a big music fan. He sang and played guitar in a band called Rock Bottom Remainders with fellow authors Dave Barry, Barbara Kingsolver, and Amy Tan, among others. The group, which was active from 1992 to 2012, performed at a number of venues across the country, including the Miami Book Fair and New York City’s Webster Hall. They also played at several charity events over the years.

ACCIDENT

In June 1999, Stephen King was seriously injured after being hit by a van while walking in Maine. He suffered several injuries and spent three weeks in the hospital. After another surgery, he slowly recovered with the help of his wife. Despite the accident, King returned to writing later that same month.
